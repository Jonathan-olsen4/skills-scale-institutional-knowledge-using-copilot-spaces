# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Test Engineer

### Role Summary
QA/Test Engineers guide test strategy and validate that software meets acceptance criteria and release quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy and plan regression coverage
- Validate acceptance criteria through functional and exploratory testing
- Document defects and verify fixes
- Assess release quality gates and report unresolved quality risks

### Goals
- Prevent defects from reaching customers
- Provide clear evidence of release quality and remaining risks
- Maintain reliable, repeatable regression coverage

### Typical Communication
- Partner with Developers on testability, automated coverage, and defect triage
- Clarify acceptance criteria and expected behavior with Product Managers
- Review quality milestones, test progress, and release blockers with Project Managers

---

## Engineering Manager / Tech Lead

### Role Summary
Engineering Managers / Tech Leads guide technical delivery, support Developers, and align engineering decisions with product and project goals.

### Responsibilities
- Set technical direction and architecture guardrails
- Guide implementation standards and technical reviews
- Identify technical delivery risks and propose mitigations
- Mentor Developers and assess engineering capacity

### Goals
- Deliver sustainable technical solutions
- Build team capability and shared engineering standards
- Make technical risks and capacity constraints visible early

### Typical Communication
- Coach Developers through design reviews and implementation decisions
- Align scope and technical trade-offs with Product Managers
- Review capacity, dependencies, and technical risks with Project Managers during planning and status updates

---

## UX Designer / Researcher

### Role Summary
UX Designers / Researchers investigate user needs and shape usable, accessible experiences through research, design, and validation.

### Responsibilities
- Research user needs and validate problem assumptions
- Define user flows, interaction designs, and prototypes
- Conduct usability testing and incorporate findings
- Define accessibility considerations and UX acceptance checks

### Goals
- Improve usability and accessibility
- Reduce uncertainty through early user feedback
- Keep delivered experiences aligned with validated user needs

### Typical Communication
- Frame problems and share research findings with Product Managers
- Review design feasibility and implementation details with Developers
- Coordinate research milestones and design handoffs with Project Managers
- Partner with QA/Test Engineers on usability and accessibility acceptance checks

---

## DevOps / Platform Engineer

### Role Summary
DevOps / Platform Engineers provide reliable delivery pipelines, environments, and operational foundations for software releases.

### Responsibilities
- Maintain CI/CD reliability and deployment automation
- Prepare and maintain development, test, and production environments
- Establish observability, alerting, and operational runbooks
- Support release verification, rollback readiness, and incident response

### Goals
- Enable repeatable, low-risk deployments
- Improve service reliability and recovery time
- Reduce environment and pipeline bottlenecks

### Typical Communication
- Work with Developers on pipeline integration, deployment requirements, and production diagnostics
- Align reliability needs and operational trade-offs with Product Managers
- Review environment readiness, release dependencies, and deployment windows with Project Managers
- Coordinate incident response and recovery updates with Developers and affected stakeholders

---

## Security/Compliance Representative

### Role Summary
Security/Compliance Representatives guide security risk assessment and policy compliance, making required controls and sign-off expectations clear throughout delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Review threats, security risks, and applicable compliance requirements
- Define required controls, compliance checks, and supporting evidence
- Clarify security sign-off requirements and assess release readiness against them
- Track unresolved findings and escalate risks through agreed paths

### Goals
- Identify and mitigate security and compliance risks early
- Make compliance constraints and approval requirements transparent
- Support secure releases with documented evidence

### Typical Communication
- Review secure implementation and remediation plans with Developers and DevOps / Platform Engineers
- Clarify compliance constraints and scope implications with Product Managers
- Coordinate review milestones, sign-off dependencies, and risk escalation paths with Project Managers

---

## Customer Support / Success Liaison

### Role Summary
Customer Support / Success Liaisons connect customer experiences with delivery teams, ensuring customer feedback informs planning and release communications.

### Responsibilities
- Capture customer pain points and recurring support themes
- Feed production issues and customer feedback into roadmap discussions
- Provide reproduction details and customer impact during issue triage
- Validate release communications, known issues, and support readiness

### Goals
- Strengthen the customer feedback loop
- Reduce handoff gaps between support and delivery teams
- Help customers understand and adopt changes

### Typical Communication
- Share prioritization signals and customer outcomes with Product Managers
- Provide Developers with issue reproduction context and validate customer-facing resolutions
- Align stakeholder communication cadence and release support handoffs with Project Managers
